# Habit-tracker

Habit-tracker is an app to manage and track your habits, chores and routine tasks.
The app helps you to keep your does in one place, watch your progress in streaks and check habits you've done in period.
You may choose time ranges for your habit you needed. For instance: daily, weekly and etc.
